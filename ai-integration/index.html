The bigger option

A rollout for the whole company

Person by person, workflow by workflow, in four phases.

Assess

  • An hour with leadership: how the company is set up, the tools in use, the pain points, and where AI is already being used without a plan.
  • 3 to 5 internal champions picked to carry it inside the company.
  • A risk read, tool recommendations, an estimate and a proposal.
Phase 1 · Assess

Intake

  • 60 to 90 minutes with each person. Their work sorted into five kinds: tasks to delegate, manual, repetitive, research, and math and data.
  • A conversation to catch the workflows the form missed.
  • A setup matched to each person's role and technical level.
  • Out of it: a record for each person, a risk report on unmanaged AI use, and a tool plan by role.
Phase 2 · Intake

Ops Engagement

  • The company layer first: enterprise agreements, zero data retention, permissions and usage policies.
  • A personal setup for each person, based on their intake.
  • Training with each person, using their actual workflows.
  • Workflows built for the company, for each role and for each person, all tested before handoff.
Phase 3 · Ops Engagement

Ongoing

  • Monthly spend reports by tool, team and user.
  • Quarterly usage reviews: who's active, who's idle, where seats should move.
  • Workflows reviewed and updated on every model release.
  • A quarterly briefing for leadership: what's paying off, where to expand, and onboarding for new hires.
Phase 4 · Ongoing

Intake and setup is a flat fee, known before we start. Ongoing work is billed for actual hours. No retainers for work that isn't happening, no minimums, and no long contracts unless you want one.

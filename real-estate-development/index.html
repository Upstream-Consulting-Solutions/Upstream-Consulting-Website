An RFI. A change order. A lease clause. A spec section that doesn't match the drawings. Each one needs a careful read against the contract, and each one lands on someone who's already stretched.

We build reviews that do that careful read with your team. Each one walks your coordinator through the call a senior project manager would make, one question at a time.

A person decides. The review never does.

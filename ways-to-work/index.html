Good to know

  • All prices are in Canadian dollars. The Working Session is $250 flat, tax included. Everything else is plus GST.
  • Getting Started starts at $1,500 plus GST and includes the working session.
  • Claude seats are billed by Anthropic to your own account.
  • The Intake is a fixed fee for the stated hours. Work beyond that bills at $250/hr and is flagged before it's incurred. The Demo is a fixed scope.
  • Build & Install and Built for You each require a 50% deposit before the build starts.
  • Built for You is quoted per tool: one flat build fee that includes the setup hours, then a monthly fee for the three month rollout. After that there is no monthly fee from us. Running costs, such as map or image services, run on your own accounts.
  • No standing discounts or credits.
